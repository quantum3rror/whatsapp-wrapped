Setup

Three steps to your Wrapped

01

Back up your iPhone

Create an unencrypted iOS backup using Finder, iTunes, or Apple Devices on your Mac or PC.

02

Open WhatsApp Wrapped

Select the year you want to analyse and pick the backup. The app finds your WhatsApp data automatically.

03

Enjoy your Wrapped

Your personal Wrapped slides open — scroll through stats, charts, and your chat personality type.

Why WhatsApp Wrapped

Built with privacy in mind
